# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Each world can keep its own `Fertilizer` and `Amount`. With Exiled's Toolkit 0.17.0 or newer, pick them for a new world in Customizable Difficulty, or change them for the world you are playing on the Mod Manager's Mods screen.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.1.1

- Fixed: the toilet gave nothing when another mod changed its fertilizer in a way the game can't use.

## 0.1.0

- The toilet gives 4 Premium Organic Fertilizer instead of 2 Basic Fertilizer.
- `Fertilizer` and `Amount` settings choose the fertilizer and how many (1 to 20).
- Research and other rewards that give the same fertilizer as the toilet don't change.
