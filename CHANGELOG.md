# Changelog

## 0.2.3

- Clearer wording in the settings and on the Mods screen.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.2.2

- Now needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.19.0 or newer.
- Settings you change on the Mods screen now always take effect right away.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.2.1

- A new install now gives the normal 2 Basic Fertilizer until you change it. To get the old defaults back, set `Fertilizer` to `PremiumOrganic` and `Amount` to `4`.
- Settings you already saved are kept.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.2.0

- Each world can now have its own `Fertilizer` and `Amount` (needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.17.0 or newer). Pick them for a new world in Customizable Difficulty, or change them for the world you're playing on the Mod Manager's Mods screen.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.1.1

- Fixed: the toilet gave nothing when another mod changed its fertilizer in a way the game can't use.

## 0.1.0

- The toilet gives 4 Premium Organic Fertilizer instead of 2 Basic Fertilizer.
- New `Fertilizer` and `Amount` settings let you pick the fertilizer and how many you get (1 to 20).
- Research and other rewards that give the same fertilizer as the toilet don't change.
